# Product photos

Put product photos here, named by part number and photo number:

    FN-C05-1.jpg   ← first photo of part FN-C05 (shown in the catalogue)
    FN-C05-2.jpg   ← second photo
    FB-60-1.png

JPG, PNG and WebP work, in any size (1000 px wide or more is best). The build makes
small and large WebP and JPG versions automatically. Products without photos show
their technical drawing instead.

Only use photos you have the right to use, such as photos from your own supplier.
